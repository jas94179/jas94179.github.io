Memory, in your control

AI assistants remember things about you, but you can't see what. That makes personalisation feel like surveillance. I designed how people see, fix and limit what an assistant remembers.

Role
Research, UX, AI experience, UI, prototyping
Timeline
4 weeks, part time · 18 screens
  • 1Control lives in the chat: every new memory shows up with Undo
  • 2Answers show which memories they used, with Edit and Forget
  • 3The assistant asks before saving anything sensitive
Outcome
4 of 5testers felt in control
5 of 5found and fixed a memory

How I work

AI speeds me up. Judgement stays mine.

I use AI where it speeds things up, and design by hand where craft matters. Everything is reviewed before it reaches a user.

1

Understand

Talk to users, read the raw notes, and find the real problem.

2

How I use AI responsibly

Fast with AI. Careful with people.

Speed only matters if people can trust the result. These five rules apply to my own work and to every AI product I design.

Check against the sourceAI summaries are a start. Raw notes and real data are the truth.
Every summary
Keep sensitive data outNo client or personal data goes into public AI tools.
Always
Show confidence and sourcesPeople should know when to trust an answer and when to check it.
In every design
Humans approve big actionsAgents ask before anything risky or hard to undo.
In every agent
I review everythingNothing AI-made reaches a user without my review.
Before shipping
